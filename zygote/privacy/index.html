Zygote — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

What we collect

Zygote collects two things: a random device ID that the app creates when you first open it, and your game state (your organism, resources and progress). You don't give us your name, email or any account. The device ID isn't linked to your identity.

Why

Only to run your game. The server uses the device ID to find your game and keeps your game state so it can advance while you're away.

Where it's stored

On our own server, at zygote.lastdog.app. The app talks to it over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Tracking and ads

Zygote has no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics.

Deleting your data

To have your game deleted from our server, email pjkirner@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
