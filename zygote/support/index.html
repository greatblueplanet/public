Zygote — Support

Zygote is an idle game for iPhone and iPad: you grow a living organism one hex cell at a time.

Questions, problems or ideas? Email pjkirner@gmail.com and I'll get back to you.

Common questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes. Your game is saved on our server and tied to this device. If you delete the app, you lose access to that game; linking an Apple account, so you can keep it, is coming in a later update.

Do I need an internet connection?
Yes. Your game runs on our server, so Zygote needs to be online to play.

How do I reset my game?
Open the Settings screen and tap “Reset game to start”, then confirm.
